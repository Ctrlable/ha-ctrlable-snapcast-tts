How routing works

  1. When an ESP32 satellite triggers TTS, it sends its satellite ID and (optionally) the wake word
  2. The add-on looks up the mapping row for that satellite + wake word (or falls back to the * wildcard row)
  3. Audio streams to the matched Snapcast client(s), switching their source temporarily and restoring it after
